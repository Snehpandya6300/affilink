Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026. This policy describes how the Affilink demo handles account and activity data.

Data we store

  • Account data: your name, email address, account type and a scrypt hash of your password. We never store plain-text passwords.
  • Profile data you choose to provide: website, social links, niche, audience size, country, promotional methods and avatar URL.
  • Activity data: saved programs, application tracking entries you create, program views and outbound application clicks.
  • Session data: a random session token stored in an HTTP-only cookie, with an expiry date and the account it belongs to.

Analytics and cookies

We record program page views and outbound clicks so brands can see whether a listing performs. Views are deduplicated per visitor for 12 hours and associated with an irreversible hash derived from IP address and user agent — we do not store raw IP addresses for analytics. Administrators and the owning brand are excluded from view counts.

The only cookie we set is the session cookie described above, marked HTTP-only and SameSite=Lax. We do not use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Third-party destinations

Application buttons on program pages send you to external websites operated by brands or affiliate networks. Those destinations have their own privacy policies and may set their own cookies. Reading the destination's policy before applying is recommended.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, remove saved programs and delete application tracking entries at any time from your dashboard. Contacting us lets you request account deletion; in this demo, deleting an account removes the associated profile, saved programs and application records.

For questions about this policy, use the contact form.
