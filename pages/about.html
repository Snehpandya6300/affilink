A cleaner way to find affiliate programs.

Affilink exists because choosing a program shouldn't require ten tabs, three spreadsheets and a guess about cookie length.

What Affilink does

Affilink is a directory of affiliate programs. Creators and publishers search by niche, compare commission structures and payout terms, and save the programs they want to pursue. Brands list their program once and get discovered by people already looking for something to promote.

We deliberately stay out of the middle. Applications happen on the brand's official affiliate page or network. We don't approve creators, hold commissions or influence payouts.

How listings are handled

Submission
A brand account submits program details. Everything enters a pending queue.
Review
An administrator approves the listing or rejects it with a written reason.
Publication
Only approved programs appear in search, category pages and the sitemap.
Edits
Brand changes go back through review so terms don't quietly change.

About this demo

Program listings cover real, currently open affiliate programs. Each one links to the brand's official application page and records the date its commission, cookie and payout terms were checked. Brand names and logos belong to their owners — Affilink is an independent directory and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a partner of those brands.

Affiliate terms change frequently. Where a brand does not publish a figure, the listing says “Not published” instead of estimating, and you should always confirm current terms on the brand's page before promoting.

Accounts, saved programs, creator affiliate links and analytics are stored in a local SQLite database for this demonstration build.
