FAQ for brands

Listing questions.

What does it cost?
Listing is free in this demo. There is no payment flow, no billing details and no commitment.
Does Affilink handle applications or payouts?
No. Every "Apply" button sends creators to your official affiliate page or network. You keep managing approvals, tracking and payments.
Can I edit a live listing?
Yes. Open the listing in your dashboard, update any field and save. It returns to the review queue so changes are checked before they go live again.
What data can I see?
Listing views, outbound application clicks, creator saves and derived click-through rate, plus a 30-day trend for each program.
