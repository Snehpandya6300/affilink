FAQ

Questions, answered.

Everything you need to know before you start applying or listing.

Is Affilink free to use?
Yes. Browsing programs and creating a creator account is free. Brands can submit a program for review at no cost.
Does Affilink approve my affiliate application?
No. Affilink is a discovery platform. When you click "Apply", we send you to the brand's official affiliate network or signup page. Approval is entirely up to the brand.
How are commissions and cookie durations verified?
Each listing links to the brand's own affiliate page and records the date its commission, cookie and payout terms were checked (November 2026). Programs change their terms often, so confirm the current numbers on the brand's page before promoting. Where a brand doesn't publish a figure, the listing says "Not published" rather than guessing.
Can I add my own Amazon or brand affiliate link?
Yes. In your creator dashboard, open My Affiliate Links, paste the affiliate link from Amazon Associates, Etsy, Booking.com or any brand, and Affilink gives you a short shareable link that counts clicks and sends visitors to the brand with your tracking intact. The brand pays you directly — Affilink never takes a cut.
What commission types are supported?
Percentage revenue share, recurring commission, fixed CPA bounties and tiered structures. You can filter by any of them in the directory.
Can I save programs and track applications?
Yes. Create a creator account to save programs, log applications and update their status (pending, approved, rejected or withdrawn) as you hear back from brands.
How do I list my brand's program?
Create a brand account, submit your program details, and our team reviews it. Approved listings become publicly visible in the directory and your dashboard records views and outbound clicks.
